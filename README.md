Outside In Due Diligence Tool: https://aflatau1.github.io/outside-in-dd/

This is a tool to perform outside in due diligence on target companies for private equity clients. It can also auffice for annual monitoring of portfolio companies without access to management. 

By entering the target company name, industry, annual revenue, regions of operation, a time period, and selecting the ESG topic areas to assess, the tool generates an an investment-committee-ready due diligence memo on the target.

The report opens with an executive summary and materiality matrix, then for each material topic delivers a five-part assessment: a risk rating with the driving factor, incidents (with cost and remediation details), the regulatory landscape (both forward-looking, which regulations may apply, their thresholds, compliance requirements, noncompliance consequences, and active enforcement actions against the company, if any), as well as the company's capacity to manage that risk. It closes with recommended follow-up questions. Every claim carries an inline citation linking to the public source it came from, and the whole memo is exportable as a structured JSON object, a Markdown file, an editable Word document, or a printable PDF.

Improvements will be made by adding context of proprietary knowledge on ESG topics and our report style to enhance the outputs. It would also benefit from a paid API.
